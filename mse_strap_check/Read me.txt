MSE STRAP CLEARANCE - BOTH END BENTS
13 October 2026

Open "MSE Strap Clearance - Both End Bents.bpad" in Blockpad and recalculate.
The first tab has shared inputs. The next two tabs have live plan drawings,
wall/pile route selectors and all-origin results. Amber equations are editable.
Expand EditableGeometry in each bent tab to change wall lines and pile offsets.

Starting inputs: 2 in trial strap, 0 in required pile gap, 0 in placement
tolerance, 15-degree test angle, 38 ft trial layer, ordinary minimum length.
These are explicit trial inputs, not confirmed vendor dimensions or tolerances.
The current model contains 18 in square, plumb piles. Each strap anchor is the
perpendicular projection of the chosen pile center onto the BACK of facing.

Both signs are checked against every pile and the full nearby wall segments.
The plan shows the cap projection, but the default layer is below the cap.
Changing TestAngle checks a different straight route; no dog-leg is assumed.
Max width and extra separation are target-only geometric outputs. Final vendor
length, connections, design clearance, tolerances, coping height, battered-pile
envelopes and all reinforcement layers require separate coordination.

STARTING RESULT
Front wall maximum nominal strip width at 15 degrees:
  EB1: 1.8598 in governing across four piles.
  EB2: 1.5674 in governing across four piles.
A 2 in strip therefore fails both front-wall target checks. Return wall routes
also fail; their nearest piles require roughly 36.1-36.2 degrees for this width.
Front cap setbacks are 25.18 / 24.62 in, but cap footprints overlap the return
wall lines about 2 in. These are separate conditions, not an overall layout pass.
