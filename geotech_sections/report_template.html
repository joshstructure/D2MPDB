B4 Pedestrian Bridge — geotechnical sections

Vertical planes parallel to stationing, through the registered FreeCAD model and the existing-ground survey. Hover a section to read station and elevations; scroll to zoom, drag to pan, double-click to reset.

Sources and limits
  • Structure: registered FreeCAD model, SHA-256 … (finished components, MSE leveling pads and piles). Piles within 15 ft of a plane are drawn dashed as projected.
  • Terrain profiles are exact cuts of the supplied LandXML triangles placed by the verified registration of the CAD tooling; no re-triangulation or smoothing. Gaps in a profile are gaps in the survey.
  • Elevations in feet as carried by the model and the surveys. No soil layers, borings or water table are shown. Preliminary — not for construction.
