B4 viewing palette — 15 October 2026

Open the bridge, then run Apply_Bridge_Colors.FCMacro. It applies colors to
the active B4 bridge without saving, recomputing, or changing visibility or
transparency. Save in FreeCAD when you want to retain the display changes.
Reapply after creating new reinforcement to color its new objects.

Color represents component or bar family, never clash or approval status.
Sources, paths, contact tools, and annotations are excluded. Hidden physical
components receive the palette but remain hidden. Dimensions, placements,
native controls, quantities, and outstanding review issues are unchanged.
