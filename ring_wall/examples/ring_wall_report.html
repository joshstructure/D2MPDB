Purpose and load path
Design the initial steel cage, then investigate ring-supported wall bands using an equilibrated tied arch between each pair of piles. Concrete carries compression; dedicated horizontal ties close arch thrust; dedicated vertical collectors lift tributary concrete weight into the arch. The ring is a finite steel bearing surface. An assumed concrete cone does not shorten the pile-center span or establish breakout capacity. The wall-end strips deliver vertical force and a couple to the outer rings and require their own local load-path verification.

Compatibility and lower-wall suspension
The band assignment partitions all wall weight exactly once. It is a proposed strength load path, not a prediction of actual row load sharing. Rings may engage differently because of settlement, shrinkage, concrete cracking, pile deformation or erection sequence. The lowest band includes all concrete below the lowest ring: collectors must carry that weight upward and be anchored into the compression field. A support cone or equal division by the number of rings cannot establish compatibility.

3 · Equilibrated tied arches between piles

Discrete distributed-load model
Each bay uses 4 straight compression segments on a parabolic polygon. Tributary column loads act at each joint, with half-width columns at the two endpoints. Moving self-weight vertically to these joints preserves external moments but requires an internal collector path: full tributary column weight is conservatively assigned as vertical tie demand at interior joints. Do not move the whole bay load to one crown without those collectors. Horizontal base ties are centered on the entered reinforcement elevation and resist thrust independently in every bay; no cancellation between neighboring arches or lateral pile fixity is credited.

The planar diagram passes through the pile center only as an equivalent representation. Actual compression must flow around the hollow steel pipe. The entered equivalent concrete width is bounded by wall thickness minus pipe OD. Half of the reduced annular bearing area is assigned to each adjacent bay. This mapping needs a three-dimensional node model; it is not an established effective area. The conservative ν = 0.45 avoids claiming higher node efficiency while local tie directions and confinement are unresolved. Every concrete strength result is therefore labeled SCREEN.

How to study rings at different vertical spacings
Change ring count, first ring elevation and spacing together, then rerun. The notebook regenerates all band heights and checks shallow top/bottom bands, minimum bar spacing, arch fit, angles and ring forces. Increasing spacing raises per-ring gravity and may provide more arch depth. Adding rows does not by itself prove that their reactions share equally. A short terminal band can govern the entire uniform cage.
