Compatibility and lower-wall suspension
The band assignment partitions all wall weight exactly once. It is a proposed strength load path, not a prediction of actual row load sharing. Rings may engage differently because of settlement, shrinkage, concrete cracking, pile deformation or erection sequence. The lowest band includes all concrete below the lowest ring: collectors must carry that weight upward and be anchored into the compression field. A support cone or equal division by the number of rings cannot establish compatibility.
